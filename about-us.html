About us

The first Chromium browser built to protect your privacy.

Epic has removed all Google tracking and blocks hundreds of companies from tracking you, with extensive anti-fingerprinting protection and the Epic ad blocker.

Our vision

What you search and browse online should always be private by design.

You should not have to trust us with your data. Instead, we should not have access to your data to the maximum extent possible. For example, autofill in Epic is done via a local database, so Epic never sends what you type in the address bar to any server.

A short history.

2013

Epic Privacy Browser

Epic launches the first Chromium-based browser engineered for privacy, with Google tracking removed and tracker blocking built in.

2020

Mobile

Epic arrives on iOS and Android, with the same tracker, ad and fingerprint blocking.

Today

Still no catch

Over a decade of transparency, security and privacy. No ad network, no rewards token, no data deals.

The company

Hidden Reflex

The Epic Privacy Browser is a product from Hidden Reflex, a software product company founded by Alok Bhardwaj with a development team based in Bangalore, India. Graham Holdings (formerly the Washington Post Company) and a small group of angel investors back us.

Rob Malda, the founder of Slashdot, and technology writer and privacy advocate Douglas Rushkoff are advisors.

Founder

Alok Bhardwaj

Alok grew up in Texas, Virginia and Kansas, and studied philosophy and music at Princeton University. He has worked as an advisor in finance for auto parts, media and tech companies, as a telecommunications researcher, and as a teacher in New York City. He began programming on an NEC computer running CP/M nearly three and a half decades ago and is a strong privacy advocate.
