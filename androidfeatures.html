Epic for Android

Epic's privacy features, in your pocket.

Enjoy Epic's privacy features on Android devices.

Get it on Google Play →Download the APK

Not able to install from the Play Store? Download the APK. You may need to modify the settings in your Android device to allow installation of apps from outside the Play Store.

What's included.

01

Built on Chromium

For speed and security.

02

File Vault

Encrypt any files you download or store on your Android device to protect your privacy.

03

AdBlocker

Install it for free via the Epic Extensions store. Epic was the first browser to block cryptomining scripts and now offers that protection to Android users. Epic's AdBlocker blocks ads, trackers, cryptomining scripts, popups and more.

04

Audio Queue

On the road? Going for a run? Add webpages to Epic's audio queue and Epic will read the articles to you. Epic is the first-ever web browser to use Android's text-to-speech support for this accessibility feature.

05

Fingerprinting Protection

Epic blocks multiple fingerprinting techniques used by data collectors.

06

Encrypted Connection Preference

Epic tries to connect to websites using an encrypted connection whenever possible.

07

Close All Tabs & Delete Data

An easy, menu-based option that clears everything in one tap.

08

Granular, Site-Based Privacy Controls

If a site isn't working, you can disable ad & tracker blocking (if you've installed AdBlocker) as well as other privacy protections. If a site is slow or questionable, you can disable scripts for a site (an advanced setting that may suppress some or all site functionality).

09

Tracker Count

See how many trackers are blocked in your browsing sessions when the AdBlocker is installed (typically thousands!).

10

Bookmarks and Passwords

Bookmark support, plus optional password saving for sites of your choice.

11

Reader Mode

Convert pages to text-only for easy reading.

12

Customized Dials

Set each dial on Epic's new tab page to one of your choice. No browsing history to report on your "most visited sites".

Try Epic. We think you'll love the faster, more private and convenient "Epic" browsing experience.
